Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 26, 2023

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you use the SalahApp mobile application (the “App”).

1. Information Collection and Use

Location Data

To provide accurate prayer times based on your current location, the App requests access to your device’s location services (both fine and coarse location).

  • Purpose: This data is used solely to calculate prayer times using the Adhan library.
  • Storage: Location coordinates (latitude and longitude) may be stored locally on your device in the App’s private preferences to maintain functionality when you are offline or when the App is restarted.
  • Sharing: We do not sell or share your precise location data with third parties for their own marketing purposes, except as described in the “Advertising” section below.

Device Information

The App may collect certain information automatically, including the type of mobile device you use, your mobile device’s unique device ID, the IP address of your mobile device, your mobile operating system, and information about the way you use the App.

2. Advertising

The App uses third-party advertising services to serve advertisements. These services may collect and use information about your visits to this and other apps in order to provide advertisements about goods and services of interest to you.

  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob for advertising. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this App and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Privacy Link: For more information on how Google uses your data, please visit Google’s Privacy & Terms.

3. Data Retention

We will retain locally stored data (such as coordinates) for as long as the App is installed on your device. You can clear this data at any time by clearing the App’s cache/data in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

4. Security

The security of your information is important to us. We implement standard security measures to protect the information stored on your device. However, remember that no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the “Last Updated” date.
