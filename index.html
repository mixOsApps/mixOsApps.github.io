Salah App

A step-by-step prayer guide for new Muslims.

  • Every prayer walked through one step at a time — niyyah, takbir, Al-Fatiha, ruku’, sujood, through to the end
  • Prayer times for Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha, Witr and Qiyam, calculated on your device from your location
  • Qibla direction
  • Reference guides, such as wudu — what is required, what is recommended, and what breaks it
  • Where the four schools of law differ, all four positions are shown side by side, with your selected school marked
  • Every ruling names the source it comes from
